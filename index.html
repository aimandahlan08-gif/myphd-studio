Voice

The studio voice runs on your own computer, using its graphics chip when the browser allows it. The first time, it downloads a voice model of about 310 MB (or 90 MB without a usable graphics chip). After that it loads from your browser's storage.

Copy the script, generate one recording of it in VoiceStudio or any voice app, then load the audio file. Scenes follow the recording in proportion to their word counts.
